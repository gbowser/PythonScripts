SCOPE AND TERMINOLOGY
- Masking algorithms: SEP and MTObjects (MTO).
- Both algorithms operate on the original science image.
- Spike Gate operates on the residual image and identifies candidate foreground interference along the deprojected bar-major profile.
- Phase 2 converts Spike Gate from an objective-only reward into a component selector: only connected mask components credibly associated with the gate can enter the final mask.
- Connected components are labelled in native science-image coordinates and projected once into the common deprojected diagnostic view.
- A retained component must intersect the compact gate target, have at least 20% of its projected pixels inside the wider permitted support region, and occupy no more than 8% of the diagnostic view.
- Masked profile samples are replaced by a log-linear bridge for profile diagnostics.
- A zero or near-zero detection is penalised when credible gate candidates exist.

AUDIT DESIGN
- Fifteen gate-positive, high-risk galaxies were used as a stress-test audit.
- The audit used short 24-trial searches for each algorithm.
- SEP and MTO parameter searches operated on science images; gate evidence came from residual images.
- Full four-fold and 182-galaxy execution was conditional on passing the audit.

SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION
- Phase 2 sharply reduces damage to coherent galaxy morphology.
- Low masked area is evidence of conservatism, not by itself proof of correct recovery.
- MTO is presently the stronger candidate for Spike Gate profile protection.
- The result should not be described as a complete foreground-object catalogue because 30 gate-positive galaxies received no accepted mask.
- SEP should remain excluded from a production-style Phase 2 batch until its science-image segmentation can generate compact gate-associated components reliably.

IMPROVEMENT OPTIONS - PRIORITISED
1. Gate credibility model.
   Quantify spike amplitude, side-drop strength, width, residual compactness, persistence across profile widths, and agreement with a 2-D residual component. Distinguish credible foreground candidates from galaxy-structure spikes before applying the zero-detection penalty.
2. Native-coordinate target/support masks.
   Construct gate support directly in native image coordinates, or transform it once with traceable area conservation, so recovery, precision, excess area and protected loss use the same denominator.
3. Candidate-specific component association.
   Assign each gate candidate to the best intersecting MTO component and report matched, unmatched, split and merged candidates. Optimise candidate-level recall and precision rather than only aggregate pixel overlap.
4. Morphology protection.
   Penalise components with large azimuthal span, annular coherence, tangential elongation along spiral arms, or strong overlap with low-frequency galaxy/isophotal structure.
5. Two-stage recovery for misses.
   Use conservative Phase 2 MTO first. For credible unmatched gates only, run a constrained local recovery search in a small native-coordinate window, with tighter area and morphology caps.
6. Manual labelled validation set.
   Label approximately 20-30 galaxies, including NGC4020, NGC4532, NGC5236 and high-discrepancy examples. Record true foreground regions, galaxy structure, uncertain regions and profile relevance.
7. Cross-validation redesign.
   Stratify folds by image scale, inclination, galaxy morphology, gate count and foreground density. Report uncertainty intervals and fold-to-fold parameter stability.
8. Runtime controls.
   Cap raw component count, add per-image time/memory limits and checkpoint/resume. NGC5236 required 4m17s and generated 11,537 raw segments.

PROPOSED ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA FOR NEXT ITERATION
- Mean credible-candidate recovery >= 0.70.
- Candidate detection >= 0.75.
- No unexplained zero detections for high-credibility candidates.
- Mean protected galaxy loss <= 0.01.
- Mean native-image masked fraction <= 0.02; per-image maximum <= 0.05.
- No visually confirmed removal of coherent spiral arms, rings or bar structure in the labelled audit set.
- Stable performance across four stratified folds.
