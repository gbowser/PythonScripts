What this tool does

Uses the existing FITS pixels and ellipse fits. Interval and threshold changes are exploratory. It does not refit ellipses or overwrite the pipeline manifest, images or reports.

The 48 examples span different severities and threshold boundaries. They are not a random sample for estimating population percentages.

Grey measurements are excluded from the pipeline geometry. Neighbouring ellipses are correlated; sample count is not independent statistical evidence.

Selected disc evidence

These are review screens, not calibrated confidence tests. Disabling a warning does not establish that a measurement is reliable.

Explore the review thresholds

Nearly-face-on discs (b/a > 0.90) are exempt from sampling and PA-variation flags. Their PA is geometrically ambiguous, rather than a quality defect.

Class A and B bar evidence

Common screen: at least three accepted bar ellipses, peak ellipticity ≥0.20, PA scatter ≤10°, and an outward ellipticity drop ≥0.08, or a drop ≥0.04 with an outer PA change ≥10°. Class A additionally requires peak ellipticity ≥0.30, drop ≥0.10 and scatter ≤5°. B passes the common screen but misses at least one A threshold. The selected-interval diagnostic here is not a new automated bar search, and neither class confirms a physical bar.
