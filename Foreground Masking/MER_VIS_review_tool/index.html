What this tool does

Uses the existing FITS pixels and ellipse fits. Interval and threshold changes are exploratory. The centre retry refits ellipses into a separate review folder. It does not overwrite the pipeline manifest, images or reports.

The examples span different severities and threshold boundaries. They are not a random sample for estimating population percentages.

Grey measurements are excluded from the pipeline geometry. Neighbouring ellipses are correlated; sample count is not independent statistical evidence.

Update centre and recompute

Click Pick centre, then click the galaxy nucleus. Yellow + is your proposed centre. The new fit uses the native FITS pixels and recomputes ellipses, disc/bar selection, angles, inclination and quality flags.

Coordinates are zero-based in the original FITS array; X increases right, Y increases upwards in this viewer.

When fixed, zero centre scatter is imposed by the constraint; it is not evidence that the centre has been independently validated.

Download refit metrics CSV

A retry is stored separately. Original results and FITS files are preserved. It may take several minutes.

Brightness presets

Same monochrome AutoSTF-style method as the earlier toy-object tool. Statistics are recalculated for the current view. Gamma = 1 gives the base AutoSTF; other gamma values add an extra adjustment. Min/max are calculated automatically.

Original pipeline: our automatic Photutils ellipse-fitting results, saved in MER_VIS_local_geometry_manifest.csv before your interactive changes. These are our measurements of the FITS image, not Euclid catalogue measurements. This column stays as the original baseline even when you display a manual-centre refit.

Current selection: our values recalculated from the displayed fit using your current disc/bar intervals and ellipse sample pool. A centre refit supplies a new fitted profile; changing intervals selects from that profile. Display stretch and zoom do not affect these values.

Euclid: comparison values already stored in the manifest from the Euclid MER source catalogue. The PA is derived from its detection POSITION_ANGLE, converted to sky coordinates with the project’s adopted +90° adjustment. The inclination is a proxy inferred from the catalogue detection shape, assuming a thin circular disc; it is not a direct inclination measurement. This is a whole-source detection comparison, not an independent measurement of the outer disc. No Euclid bar PA is supplied here. The app reads these saved values; it does not query Euclid live.

Δ Selection − Euclid: current-selection value minus the Euclid comparison, in degrees. For inclination, positive means the selection is more inclined and negative means less inclined. PA differences use the shortest signed angular difference between equivalent axes (modulo 180°). An em dash means a comparison is unavailable.

Selected disc evidence

Centre acceptance uses the displayed explicit reference, in native pixels; displaced samples are excluded from the calculation. The centre limits are pilot settings for review. Other screens describe uncertainty, not calibrated confidence tests. Disabling a warning does not establish that a measurement is reliable.

Explore the review thresholds

Pilot settings: offset ≤ min(cap, max(floor, fraction × radius)). Same reference and centre limit for accepted and marginal pools. Fixed-centre fits have zero drift by constraint and still need image review.

Nearly-face-on discs (b/a > 0.90) are exempt from sampling and PA-variation flags. Their PA is geometrically ambiguous, rather than a quality defect.

Class A and B bar evidence

Common screen: at least three accepted bar ellipses, peak ellipticity ≥0.20, PA scatter ≤10°, and an outward ellipticity drop ≥0.08, or a drop ≥0.04 with an outer PA change ≥10°. Class A additionally requires peak ellipticity ≥0.30, drop ≥0.10 and scatter ≤5°. B passes the common screen but misses at least one A threshold. The selected-interval diagnostic here is not a new automated bar search, and neither class confirms a physical bar.
