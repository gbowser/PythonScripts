There are 2777 PNGs and 47 PDFs (463 pages): 2694 QI and 83 NoQI cards. The report review index maps galaxy IDs and catalogue names to PNGs and PDF pages.

For non-face-on discs, PA variation requires at least six selected accepted ellipses, no radial gap over half their span, and angular RMS scatter above 10 degrees. Otherwise sparse sampling is flagged. These are operational review rules, not calibrated confidence levels. Adjacent ellipses are correlated.

There are 227 secure positional catalogue identifications. A match requires one galaxy identity within 5 arcsec and an adopted separation <=2 arcsec. No Messier, NGC or IC identifier was returned among accepted matches. Non-matches do not imply absence from every catalogue.

The fitted angles, centres and fit outcomes are unchanged by this review. Four objects have no adopted geometry. Failed-fit images omit fallback centre markers and rejected trial ellipses. Source science FITS files are unchanged.
